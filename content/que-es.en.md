After the legendary **Night of the Five Defeats**, the Primordial Tilt shattered the harmony of Los Gallos Sin Planes. Their champions fell to corruption and the realm broke apart.

Among the ruins, a chick wakes up with no name, no rank and no memories. To find out who caused the catastrophe, it must cross a huge castle, face the legendary Gallos and decide what to do with each of them.

*El Corral: Eclipse of the Tilted* is a dark fantasy Metroidvania, demanding like a soulslike, with Dominican humor. Sometimes defeating your friends is the only way to save them.
